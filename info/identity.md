### Flagship Platform: Discover Himalayan Trails (DHT)
- **What It Is**: An independent digital travel atlas documenting high-altitude Himalayan trails across North India with authentic GPS routes, 3D digital elevation models (DEM), and verified logistical field notes.
- **The Problem It Solves**: Most mountain travel guides are commercial aggregators lacking topographic context, altitude profiles, or verified waypoints. DHT provides authentic coordinates and elevation profiles without marketing bias.
- **Ashish's Role**: Founder, Lead Designer, and Frontend Engineer.
- **Live Platform**: [discoverhimalayantrails.com](https://discoverhimalayantrails.com) (Repository: [github.com/Ashish00968/travel](https://github.com/Ashish00968/travel)).

### Pillar 1: Tech Stack & Systems Architecture (`/tech-stack`)
- **Core Engineering**: Modern component architectures, strict TypeScript interfaces, reactive state management, and zero-bloat styling.
- **3D Geospatial Systems**: Mapbox GL JS with 1.5× vertical DEM exaggeration, custom terrain shaders, and vector tile streaming.
- **Media Engine & Network Resilience**: Dynamic Cloudinary transformations, progressive LQIP blur-up placeholders preventing layout shifts on remote 3G mountain networks.

### Pillar 2: Travel & Mountain Field Notes (`/travel`)
- **Field Grounding**: Firsthand trail documentation across Himachal Pradesh (Solang Valley, Sethan / Hampta, Rohtang Pass, Kinnaur).
- **Summits & Passes**: Summited Patalsu Peak (4,261m) with verified 36-waypoint GPS recording; documented historic Rohtang Pass (3,978m).
- **Industry Collaboration**: Professional collaboration with **Trekking Hills Travels**, bridging digital interfaces with operational field logistics.

### Pillar 3: Photography & Videography (`/photography-video`)
- **Mountain Photography**: High-altitude alpenglow landscapes, high-dynamic-range alpine lighting, and fine detail preservation.
- **Color Science**: Interactive comparison demonstrating flat camera RAW / LOG profiles balanced into finished cinematic mountain stills.
- **Cinematic Video Editing**: Pacing, rhythmic cutting, beat synchronization, and ambient mountain audio Foley. Complete video reels accessible at [editzportfolio.netlify.app](https://editzportfolio.netlify.app).
